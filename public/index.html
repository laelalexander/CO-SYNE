CO-SYNE Network

Driver to Emergency Services

Three applications on one network. Open each one on its own device (a phone for the driver, the patrol car terminal for the officer, and the communications center for dispatch), or open all three on one computer to demonstrate a stop.

Driver

The driver's phone. Verified digital ID, the pull-over alert with the officer's verified identity and reason, video with the officer, tickets, and one-tap emergency.

  • Install on a phone: scan its QR code on the downloads page
Open Driver app

Officer Terminal

The patrol vehicle's console. Sticker scanner, stop console with live video, records lookup, map, CAD calls, eCitations, reports, messages, and evidence.

  • Runs full screen as the terminal's operating shell
Open Officer Terminal

Dispatch

The network itself: every stop, call, unit, and device live on the map, drop-in video, assignments, records, citations, reports, and the tamper-evident ledger.

  • Communications center console
Open Dispatch
