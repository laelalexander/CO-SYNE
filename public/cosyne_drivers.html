For drivers

Know who is stopping you, before anyone approaches.

The CO-SYNE Driver app puts a verified digital ID on your phone and alerts you the moment an officer starts a stop, with their verified name, badge, agency and the reason. You can talk from your seat, by video or by text.

Get the Driver appOpen it in your browser

Works on iPhone (iOS 16.4 or later) and Android.

Everything you need at the roadside

Pull-over alerts

Even with the app closed or your phone locked, you get the officer's verified identity and the reason, with Accept video or Text only.

Verified on your phone

Your phone checks the officer's credential against the agency's published key itself. If it fails, you get a clear warning and 911 is alerted.

Stay in your car

Talk with the officer by video and voice from your seat. Dispatch can join if needed, and the session is recorded as evidence.

Digital ID and vehicle

Your license, registration and insurance in one place, and still available offline when you have no signal.

Tickets on your phone

Citations and warnings arrive signed by the officer. Acknowledge receipt, pay, or request a court date. A receipt is not an admission.

One-tap emergency

Medical, crash, fire, danger or breakdown: your location, vehicle and ID go straight to 911, and your location keeps updating.

Accessibility

Your needs, shared before the conversation starts

Add notes such as hearing loss, autism or a medical condition to your CO-SYNE ID. Officers see them on their terminal at the start of the stop.

  • Text-only stopsChoose Text only and communicate entirely in writing, ideal for Deaf and hard-of-hearing drivers.
  • Accommodations up frontThe officer's console highlights your accommodation note next to your verified license.
  • Clear, calm guidanceSimple steps on screen: pull over safely, stay in the vehicle, keep hands visible.

About the stickers

Your privacy

Your key stays on your phone

Your ID is bound to a private key created on your phone. It is never sent to CO-SYNE, so a copied ID can't be used by anyone else.

Your sticker holds a link only

The QR code on your sticker contains a link, never your name, address or license details.

Recordings stay with you

Recordings stay on the device that made them. Only a fingerprint is stored, so any later edit can be detected.
