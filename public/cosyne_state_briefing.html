CO-SYNE · Briefing for state DMVs and DOTs

A verified roadside,
issued by your state.

A ready system to verify residents against REAL ID, issue state-signed credentials and QR sticker sets, and verify drivers across state lines.

The resident flow

From application to a verified stop.

ApplyIn the Driver app, online or at a DMV office, with the $24.99 fee authorized.
VerifyThe state confirms the applicant against the REAL ID license on file.
ApproveThe fee is charged only on approval.
IssueA state-signed credential, letter and sticker set.
MailBumper sticker, window sticker and plate tag.
StopThe officer scans; both sides are verified.
The system

The State Issuer Console.

  • Application queue with REAL ID checks and full history
  • Print center: letters and sticker sets, single or batch
  • Renewals and automatic reminders
  • Revoke and reissue; old codes stop working at once
  • Revenue by month and county, with CSV export

Live at iobeacons.com/cosyne/state. REAL ID checks and payments are simulated until connected to state systems.

Within the REAL ID system

It builds on the verification your state already did.

REAL ID eligibilityOnly residents with a current REAL ID-compliant license can be approved.
A match, not a copyThe console asks your records whether name, birth date and license match. Your database stays yours.
Signed by the stateEach credential is signed by the state's own issuer key and expires with the membership.

CO-SYNE works alongside REAL ID. It is not endorsed or approved by DHS or any state and does not replace a license.

Interstate trust registry

Each state signs its own credentials. There is no national database.

1Each state publishes its issuer and public key to the registry.
2An officer scans a sticker from any participating state.
3The terminal checks the signature against the registry, offline if needed.
4The officer sees the issuing state. Each state can revoke its own.

Adding a state means adding an issuer, not merging records. Running today with Oklahoma and Texas demonstration issuers.

Federal and tribal partners

The same model for DHS and other agencies.

As verifiersFederal agencies, such as DHS components, could verify credentials at facilities and checkpoints.
As issuersFederal fleets and tribal nations can issue their own credentials into the registry.
One terminalFederal and tribal law enforcement verify any participating state's credentials.

Possible partnerships. No federal or tribal agency has endorsed or adopted CO-SYNE.
